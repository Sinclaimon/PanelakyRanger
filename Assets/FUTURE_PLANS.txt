SKIING MOVEMENT FUTURE PLANS (short roadmap)

- Core prototype goals
  - Focus on arcade carving feel: resistance then release.
  - Flow tiers: implicit state increasing after clean carves / sequences.
  - Minimal UI; emphasize camera, VFX (snow spray), and audio.

- Near-term tasks
  1. Base scene + input (Terrain, Capsule skier, camera, Traversal.Steer)
  2. SkierController: downhill force, lateral damping, steering torque (Rigidbody)
  3. Camera feedback: tilt/roll + subtle FOV shifts (BasicCamera.ApplyTilt)
  4. FlowState skeleton: counters for clean carves, tier API
  5. Small HUD for debugging speed + tier
  6. Procedural terrain generator & spawners (later)

- Notes
  - Keep movement & camera responsibilities separated (SRP): movement computes desired tilt, camera applies smoothing and visuals.
  - Expose tuning values in inspector for rapid iteration.
  - Use manual lateral damping (AddForce) rather than solely physics materials to control carving feel.
